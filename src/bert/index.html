Help & Shortcuts

How to use

  • Paste a few sentences and pause briefly; the score refreshes automatically.

Best results

  • Keep quotes and surrounding context together so the model sees the tone, not just isolated fragments.

Shortcuts

  • Typing reruns analysis automatically. No extra keyboard shortcuts yet.

Caveats

Scores are directional moderation signals, not context-aware rulings or policy decisions.

Privacy

Pasted text stays in this tab while the browser classifier runs.

Local only

The classifier loads in the browser, then inference happens locally on this device.
